Privacy

This game stores your progress (shards, level, unlocks, settings) in your browser's local storage on your device.

It also sends anonymous play statistics (run length, device and screen size, game version, a random player ID) to help us balance the game. We do not collect your name, email, location or any account information, and the random ID is not linked to you.

You can clear your saved data and ID at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. To opt out of statistics, contact the publisher through the game's page.
