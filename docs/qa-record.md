# Neon Bullseye: QA record (Phase 4)

*15 Nov 2026 · build 1.0.0 · `dist/crazygames/index.html` (164.6 KB, 55.4 KB zipped). Checked against `crazygames-launch.md` §3.1 and the CrazyGames requirements and quality guidelines as read on 15 Nov 2026.*

All browser checks ran in headless Chromium (Playwright) on this Mac with real input events. **Nothing here was run on a physical phone, tablet, Chromebook, Safari, Edge, or inside the CrazyGames site or app.** Those are listed at the end.

| Guideline | Result |
|---|---|

## Behaviour checks (`tests/qa.mjs`, 38 of 38 pass)

- First run from cleared storage: no console errors, no dev UI, no privacy notice, no modal; moving the mouse does not start a run; one click does; run 1 holds the ball with no timer and no damage until the player moves.
- SDK events: `init`, `loadingStart`, `loadingStop` on load; `gameplayStart` on run start and on retry; `gameplayStop` on death. Each pause path (window blur, tab hidden, pause button, P key) sends exactly one `gameplayStop`, shows a 3-2-1, then exactly one `gameplayStart`. Events strictly alternate over the session.
- Escape does nothing in play and does not close sheets. Space with a sheet open does not start a run.
- Mute: M key and the button both work; the setting survives a reload.
- Interrupted run: leaving mid-run stores the shards and XP earned so far; the next open shows "Banked N shards from your last run".
- Reduce motion: no shake, hit-stop or slow-motion. Left-handed: power-up buttons move to the right edge. Power-up key 1 works.
- Touch: one tap starts; the portrait field fills the phone width. Keyboard: W starts and steers.

## Performance

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 1920×1080, busy late-game scene, software rendering (no GPU), no throttle | 59.8 fps, p95 16.7 ms |
| 821×462, same | 60 fps |
| 1920×1080 with 2× CPU throttle | 50.8 fps; adaptive quality stepped down automatically |
| Every CrazyGames size during a normal run | 16.7 ms median; p99 16.8 ms at six sizes and 33 ms (an occasional dropped frame) at four |

The game draws about 0.3 ms of JavaScript per frame; the cost is pixel fill. The headless test has no GPU, so it is a pessimistic stand-in for a low-end laptop but not a measurement of one. If frames stay above 21 ms for 1.5 s the game lowers its render resolution (and at the lowest step drops glow effects), which is invisible on a small frame and soft but playable on a large one.

| casual | portrait | 100% | 146 s | 49 / 77 / 143 s | 6.9 |
| casual | wide (desktop) | 100% | 158 s | 52 / 85 / 153 s | 6.3 |

Before this work the novice figure was 17%. The novice bot moves the ring at thumb-drag speed with heavy aim error and never dodges; real mouse players should do better than its wide-field number. These are proxies, not forecasts.

## Not tested here: please check

1. **iPhone (Safari and the CrazyGames app):** sound starts after the first tap; sound returns after locking and unlocking the phone and tapping; nothing sits under the notch or home bar in both orientations; dragging never scrolls or zooms the page; long-press shows no magnifier or menu.
2. **Android phone (Chrome):** the same list; vibration on hits is not excessive.
3. **Low-end Chromebook (4 GB):** play to stage 4 in fullscreen. It should stay smooth; if it visibly softens after a few seconds, the adaptive quality has kicked in, which is acceptable; stutter is not.
4. **Safari and Edge on desktop:** load, play a run, open each menu.
5. **144 Hz or faster monitor:** the game should feel the same speed as at 60 Hz (the simulation test says it will).
6. **Audio by ear:** music against effects, the hit thud, the stage stinger, nothing harsh at laptop-speaker volume.
7. **CrazyGames developer preview / QA tool:** upload the zip, confirm the SDK events appear, and try the fullscreen toggle and each preview size.
8. **Name:** search "Neon Bullseye" in the CrazyGames catalogue and the app stores.
